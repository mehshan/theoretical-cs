\begin{problem}{10.21}
Let $EQ_{BP} = \{\langle B_1, B_2 \rangle \ | \ B_1 \text{ and } B_2 \text{ are equivalent branching programs} \}$. Show that $EQ_{BP}$ is coNP-complete.
\end{problem}

\begin{proof}
To show that $EQ_{BP}$ is coNP-complete, we must show that it is in coNP and that all coNP-problems are polynomial time reducible to it. $\overline{EQ_{BP}}$ is in NP as the certificate is simply an assignment of truth values to variables on which two branching programs give different outputs. Therefore, $EQ_{BP}$ is in coNP. To prove the second part, we show that $\overline{SAT}$ is polynomial time reducible to $EQ_{BP}$. The reduction converts a Boolean formula $\phi$ into two branching programs $B_1$ and $B_2$, so that $\phi$ is not satisfiable, iff $B_1$ and $B_2$ are equivalent. $B_1$ is constructed to implement $\phi$'s Boolean function. Such construction can be done in polynomial time. $B_2$ is constructed to always output 0.
\end{proof}
